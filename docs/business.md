# Business scenarios

Android release teams, consultants, enterprise APK intake teams, and security testing providers are potential users. Customer interviews, competitive assessment, and product trials would establish demand. There are no verified customers, receipts, prices, or market-validation claims in this project.

A free tier could offer capped individual scans. A professional tier could support higher limits, structured report exports, and developer integration. Team accounts, organizational audit, and enterprise reporting would need additional engineering for a business tier. The current dashboard has a local scenario calculator without billing integration.

Enter monthly active users, paying customers, monthly scans, revenue per paying customer, compute for each scan, monthly storage, infrastructure, support, percent and fixed payment fees, and new-customer acquisition costs. Each starting value is zero. Calculations assume one payment per paying customer monthly. Gross margin subtracts delivery costs including monthly support and infrastructure. Operating profit also subtracts the modeled acquisition spend. Break-even holds fixed costs, acquisition spend, and scan volume constant. Benchmarks of a small fixture cannot establish a production compute cost per scan.

Review licensing for dependencies, datasets, curated threat records, and technical evidence before offering services or distributing images. Costs and revenue should be updated with observed values when those become available.
